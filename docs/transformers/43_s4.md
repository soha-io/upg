# 43 — Efficiently Modeling Long Sequences with Structured State Spaces (S4; Gu, Goel & Ré, 2022)

**One line:** a deep sequence model whose layers are *literal linear
state-space systems* $\dot x = Ax + Bu,\ y = Cx + Du$, made trainable and
fast through the HiPPO initialization and structured (DPLR) parameterization
— the deep-learning object closest in kind to the UPG's own formalism.

## The math

Each S4 layer is a continuous-time LTI system per channel:

$$\dot x(t) = A\,x(t) + B\,u(t),\qquad y(t) = C\,x(t) + D\,u(t),$$

discretized with step $\Delta$ (bilinear/ZOH):
$\bar A = (I - \tfrac{\Delta}{2}A)^{-1}(I + \tfrac{\Delta}{2}A)$, giving the
recurrence $x_k = \bar A x_{k-1} + \bar B u_k$. Because the system is LTI,
the whole sequence map is a convolution

$$y = \bar K * u,\qquad \bar K = (C\bar B,\; C\bar A\bar B,\; C\bar A^2\bar B,\; \dots),$$

so training runs as an FFT convolution (parallel, $\mathcal{O}(L\log L)$)
while inference runs as the recurrence ($\mathcal{O}(1)$/step) — the same
duality as note 38, obtained through systems theory.

Two ingredients make it *work*:

1. **HiPPO initialization:** choose $A$ as the operator whose state
   optimally memorizes the input history under a chosen measure (projection
   of the past onto Legendre polynomials); this specific non-normal $A$
   solves the long-memory problem that random $A$ (vanishing/exploding
   $\bar A^k$) cannot.
2. **Structured parameterization:** HiPPO $A$ is normal-plus-low-rank
   (DPLR), $A = \Lambda - PQ^*$, enabling stable, cheap computation of
   $\bar K$ via Cauchy kernels rather than naive matrix powers.

Result: solved Path-X (16k-length dependency) when every transformer
variant failed; long-range dependency as *spectral structure of $A$*, not
attention span.

## What it means for the UPG

- **Same mathematical species as our model.** Our generative map — linear
  drive $\kappa B x + gu + b$ inside a saturating nonlinearity — is a
  (nonlinear) state-space system with $n = 7$ *named* states; S4 layers are
  unnamed ones. Consequences run both directions: (i) an S4-based
  forecaster is structurally well-matched to filtering our dynamics
  (note 38's sufficiency argument), making it the strongest architectural
  rival to attention for step 6 — registered as such; (ii) S4's analysis
  tools apply to *us*: our $\bar A \equiv$ Jacobian $J$, and "memory =
  spectrum of $A$" is our $\rho(J)$ critical-slowing story — S4's
  eigenvalue placement (near the unit circle for long memory) is the
  *designed* version of what approaching bifurcation does *pathologically*
  in a person.
- **The discretization bridge matters practically:** EMA occasions are
  irregular samples of continuous psychological time; S4's explicit
  $\Delta$-discretization (with learnable $\Delta$!) is the formally correct
  way to handle unequal gaps — better founded than positional-encoding
  fixes. Even without adopting S4, our Phase-4 estimators should treat the
  person as continuous-time and discretize per actual gap, exactly as
  $\bar A = \exp(\Delta A)$ does. Recorded as a Phase-4 design rule.
- **HiPPO's moral** — the right *initialization of a dynamical operator*
  encodes an entire hypothesis about memory — is rule W7 for deep models:
  our prior $\bar B$ plays HiPPO's role, initializing dynamics at the
  theory instead of at random.

The designated challenger architecture for step 6 (attention vs. SSM on the
